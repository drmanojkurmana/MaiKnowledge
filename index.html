Product walkthrough

StewardMD, screen by screen.

StewardMD is our own app, built in-house and shipped as a native iOS and Android build from a single web bundle. Every screenshot below is the real product, not a mockup. This is one clinical journey through it, from opening the app to a documented antibiotic decision.

iOS & Android · native (Capacitor) Clinician-only access On-device knowledge base first Advisory by design
01

One screen, the whole unit

Home is the clinician's hub. Jump straight into Syndromes, Ward Sync, the ICU workstation or the Antibiogram; start a structured case; or open the clinical tools — 70+ calculators and a drug and interaction reference. Cases, Search and the MaiK assistant stay one tap away in the bottom bar.

Start a Case Dx My Patient · beta 70+ calculators Drugs & interactions
02

Describe the case in plain language

Type the case the way you would say it — "65M, 2 days fever, neck stiffness, photophobia, drowsy" — and StewardMD extracts structured findings from the text. Findings become chips you can add or drop, and the engine proposes the next findings worth checking. The governing policy is selectable (here ICMR National AMRSN 2024), and the differential re-ranks live as the picture changes.

The app labels this engine beta on the screen itself and presents its score as a transparent, rule-based Clinical Confidence Score — not a validated probability.

03

An answer you can audit

The decision card gives the working diagnosis and its confidence, whether antibiotics are indicated, the disposition, and a first-line regimen down to dose, route, frequency and duration. Below it sit the time-critical safety warning, a plain-English reason for the recommendation, and the option to save the case.

One tap opens the Antimicrobial Stewardship Console: expected organisms, drug of choice, AWaRe class, ICMR 2024 alongside the local hospital antibiogram, when-to-avoid rules, an MDR-risk wizard and culture de-escalation.

04

Coverage and local resistance

A spectrum-of-activity grid maps each antibiotic class against the organisms that matter — MRSA, MSSA, streptococci, E. coli and the rest — with a second tab for resistance rates. Tap any drug or organism to isolate it.

The screen states plainly that it is a teaching guide, not a substitute for susceptibility testing or your unit's own antibiogram.

05

Ask MaiK, our clinical AI

MaiK is the clinical knowledge assistant built into the app. It answers from StewardMD's own curated knowledge base first and escalates to cloud models only when it needs to, so common questions are answered on-device. It can also hand off into a structured assessment, the drug index or a calculator.

Every answer carries an "Advisory — clinician verifies" badge and an educational-reference note. A scope gate keeps MaiK to clinical questions.

06

The ICU workstation

One patient's live picture in a single view — heart rate, blood pressure, MAP, SpO₂, respiratory rate, temperature, urine output, lactate, pressors, running infusions, net fluid balance and potassium — with dedicated tabs for haemodynamics, fluids, electrolytes, ABG and infusions.

Camera-based capture of monitor and lab data is built into the screen and marked coming soon; entry today is manual.

How it is built
A single web bundle shipped as a native iOS and Android app through Capacitor, with the clinical knowledge base packaged inside the app and a serverless API behind it.
How you get it
Access is limited to verified clinicians. StewardMD is not yet a public app-store release; builds go to clinicians for evaluation while we approach our first institutional pilots.
Validation
Each module's output goes through specialist review before it is presented as anything more than advisory. That review is targeted for December 2026.

Built by clinicians, for clinicians.

MaiKnowledge exists because the best clinical decisions deserve the best clinical intelligence — instantly, at the point of care. We started with StewardMD, an antibiotic decision engine trusted at the bedside, and we're building outward toward one connected ecosystem for safer, smarter healthcare.
