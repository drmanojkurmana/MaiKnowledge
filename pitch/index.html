Code Blue · CPR assist
How the wrist counts compressions
ON-DEVICE MOTION ONLY
THE SIGNAL CHAIN
01
Wrist motion, sampled at 50 Hz
Compressions run near two per second, so 50 Hz leaves ample headroom to resolve each one.
02
Peak detection
Each downstroke registers as a peak. The count is peaks detected, not a guess from elapsed time.
03
A repetition gate rejects single bumps
One knock against the bed rail is not a compression. A sustained rhythm is required before anything counts.
04
Rate and pause from the intervals
The gap between peaks gives compressions per minute. When peaks stop, a pause opens and is timed.
WHAT THE CLINICIAN SEES
The coach maps rate to the AHA window
Below 100 reads Faster, 100 to 120 On target, above 120 Slower. Neutral rate-matching, never a verdict on the operator.
A workout session keeps it alive
Sampling continues with the wrist down and screen off, falling back to motion alone where the health permission is unavailable.
Mirrored to an iPhone Command Center
Timer, rate, pause, counters and timeline for the team lead. Events merge by id, so a dropped link replays rather than duplicates.
Torn down when the code ends
Detector and session stop on End or ROSC. The log stays in the protected container and clears on sign-out.
WHERE THE CLAIM STOPS
Motion yields count, rate, elapsed and pause. It cannot measure depth, and the app never claims the CPR was adequate.

Constraints on the AI
What the assistant is not allowed to do
Intent firewall
The assistant answers clinical questions only. The gate is an allow-list requiring a positive medical signal, not a list of blocked topics, and it is held to zero false refusals on genuine clinical questions.
Conversation history stays on the device
The chat sidebar is scoped to the account and stored on the device, not in the cloud.
The engine decides, the AI explains
Differentials and regimens are computed deterministically. The language model explains an answer that has already been derived; it does not produce the answer.
Nothing auto-applies
Scribed and extracted fields are presented for clinician review before they populate a case. Every AI-assisted output requires explicit confirmation.
